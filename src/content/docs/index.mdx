## What it handles

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Real HDR & Dolby Vision" icon="seti:video">
    HDR10, HDR10+ (per-frame ST 2094-40), Dolby Vision (P5, P7 as single-layer 8.1, P8.1, P8.4, AV1 P10.x), and HLG, with full metadata and automatic Match Content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dolby Atmos" icon="seti:audio">
    EAC3+JOC stream-copied on every route (HDMI MAT 2.0, AirPods spatial, Bluetooth downmix), plus 5.1 / 7.1 surround with correct channel layout.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lossless height channels" icon="seti:audio">
    TrueHD and DTS:X object audio rendered into a speaker bed up to 9.1.6 and delivered as lossless 24-bit PCM, or APAC over AirPlay (optional components).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Broad codec coverage" icon="seti:default">
    H.264, HEVC and AV1 in hardware through VideoToolbox. AV1 without hardware, VP9, MPEG-2, VC-1 and everything else FFmpeg knows through the software path (dav1d, libavcodec). MKV, MP4, WebM, MPEG-TS, AVI, DVD and Blu-ray ISOs, and more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio-only path" icon="seti:audio">
    A lean pipeline with no video machinery and system Now-Playing on tvOS and iOS, driven by a single LoadOptions flag.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live TV & DVR" icon="seti:clock">
    Unbounded live with optional timeshift, and direct HLS ingest with AES-128 clear-key and SSAI ad-pod handling.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You ship the UI" icon="seti:plan">
    No AVPlayerViewController, no opinionated controls, no analytics. Bind the view, call play(), read the published state.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it compares

AVPlayer has the best OS integration but plays only Apple's formats. VLCKit and libmpv play almost everything but bypass Apple's Dolby Vision, Atmos and HDR handling. AetherEngine combines both: FFmpeg's format coverage on top of VideoToolbox and AVPlayer, so Dolby Vision, Atmos and Match Content keep working.

[Feature table and measurements](/project/comparison/) against AVPlayer, KSPlayer, VLCKit and libmpv.

## Just want to watch?

AetherEngine is the engine, not an app. If you are here to actually watch something, [Sodalite](https://sodalite.superuser404.de) is the native Apple TV client for Jellyfin built on top of it. This site is for developers integrating the engine.
